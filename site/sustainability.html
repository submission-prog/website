Responsible pest management for a more sustainable future.

Responsible pest management is about more than controlling pests. It is about finding effective solutions that consider people, properties and the environment: prevention, precision and responsible intervention.

Our first sustainability report covers 1 January to 31 December 2025 and was prepared with reference to the GRI Standards, with carbon accounting aligned to the GHG Protocol.

Environment

Pestimesh recorded energy use from petrol, diesel and electricity across its operations in FY2025. Petrol and diesel came from the fleet of vehicles that transport technicians and materials to sites, while electricity was drawn from the company's office operations.

Our people

33 employees as at 31 December 2025, across six nationalities.

Safety and training

Teams are supported through training, PPE, risk assessments, toolbox meetings and established safety procedures aligned with ISO 45001. Incidents, near misses and unsafe conditions are reported and investigated to strengthen procedures.

Commitment to the UN Sustainable Development Goals

From the treatment methods we choose to the materials used in our termite mesh installations, our work supports broader progress towards the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals. Hover over a goal to see how we contribute.

Goal 3Good health and well-being

Protecting homes and businesses from pest related health risks through safe and effective treatment methods.

Goal 6Clean water and sanitation

Careful management of water based treatments to prevent contamination of surrounding water sources.

Goal 8Decent work and economic growth

Providing stable employment and hands on technical training for our workforce of technicians and support staff.

Goal 9Industry, innovation and infrastructure

Applying Australian sourced mesh technology and innovative treatment techniques to improve service durability and effectiveness.

Goal 11Sustainable cities and communities

Supporting safe and liveable homes and workplaces through preventive pest management for the community.

Goal 12Responsible consumption and production

Selecting treatment products and methods that reduce unnecessary chemical use and environmental exposure.

Goal 15Life on land

Choosing pest control approaches that limit harm to wildlife and the surrounding ecosystem.

Governance

Pestimesh pursues business opportunities on fair and ethical terms. We do not offer, approve or accept anything of value to gain an unlawful advantage, and we do not extend gifts, travel or entertainment as a means of securing business.
