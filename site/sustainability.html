Our Heritage & Expertise

Established in 2011, Pestimesh has developed strong experience in Singapore's pest management and termite protection industry. Our portfolio includes pest control and termite-related projects for major facilities such as ICA, Singapore Zoo, Banyan Tree Hotel, Family Justice Courts, PUB Tuas Water Reclamation Plant, Project Legend at Clarke Quay, Indigo Hotel and Singapore Aviation Academy. We support developers and project management teams with pest management programmes throughout the construction phase of condominium and commercial property developments.

Specialised Termite Protection

Read more

Specialised Termite Protection

Pestimesh has developed specialised expertise in physical anti-termite protection using stainless-steel termite mesh systems. Our Project Director, Vincent Tan, strengthened his technical knowledge through specialised experience and learning from Australia, bringing practical expertise and international knowledge into termite protection projects in Singapore. We focus on prevention and long-term building protection rather than relying only on reactive treatment.

Environment & Sustainable Pest Management

Sustainability is increasingly embedded in our operations. Through Integrated Pest Management, we emphasise prevention, monitoring, targeted treatment and responsible use of pest management methods. Where practical, we seek solutions that reduce unnecessary chemical use, minimise environmental impact and provide effective, long-term protection for buildings and communities.

Environment

Pestimesh recorded energy use from petrol, diesel and electricity across its operations in FY2025. Petrol and diesel came from the fleet of vehicles that transport technicians and materials to sites, while electricity was drawn from the company's office operations.

Safety

Safety remains a top priority, given the nature of our work handling treatment chemicals and operating across varied residential, commercial and construction sites. We protect our workforce through strict safety practices, clear operating guidelines and continuous training on the proper handling of equipment and materials. Whenever incidents do occur, we investigate thoroughly to identify root causes and strengthen our procedures.

Community health and well-being

Through its community initiatives, the Company seeks to support healthier, safer and more inclusive environments while using its expertise and resources to create positive social impact.

Supporting public hygiene

Pestimesh carried out disinfection and environmental hygiene activities at Bedok Market 216, contributing to efforts to maintain a cleaner, safer and more hygienic public environment for market operators, workers and members of the public. The initiative demonstrates the Company's commitment to supporting public hygiene and community well-being, while applying its expertise in environmental hygiene and disinfection services to shared community spaces.

Promoting dengue awareness

Pestimesh conducted a dengue awareness talk to share knowledge on dengue prevention and the importance of proactive mosquito control. The session provided participants with practical information on identifying potential mosquito breeding habitats, adopting preventive measures and maintaining a clean environment. Through such awareness initiatives, Pestimesh supports greater understanding of dengue prevention and encourages individuals and communities to take an active role in reducing mosquito-related health risks.

Supporting active communities

Pestimesh is also a sponsor of the Tchoukball Association of Singapore (TBAS), supporting the development and promotion of tchoukball and greater participation in community sport. Through this sponsorship, the Company contributes to opportunities for physical activity and encourages healthy, active lifestyles, while supporting values such as teamwork, discipline and community engagement. This initiative reflects Pestimesh's broader commitment to contributing to the social well-being of the communities it serves.
